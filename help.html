Frequently asked questions and guides for using Ruduino.

Type the name of a building, block, lecture hall, or facility into the search bar on the home page. Results will show the location and relevant details. You can also use the quick-link buttons (Lecture Halls, Labs, Library…) to browse by category.
Go to the Login page and click Register here. Fill in your campus email, username, and a password (minimum 8 characters). Once submitted, your account will be created immediately.
Contact the IT Helpdesk at helpdesk@campus.edu with your campus ID and registered email. They will issue a password reset within one business day.
